e. Interest free (grace) period
   The interest free credit period could range from 45 days (Cashback/Live+ & TravelOne Credit
   Card) to 48 days (Platinum & Smart Value Credit Card)
   This means that a customer who has a billing date of 5th of the month can spend on his Card
   from 6 May to 5 June, his bill will be generated on 5 June and his Payment Due Date will be
   21 June (45 days for Cashback/Live+ Card & TravelOne Card and (24 June for Smart Value and
   Platinum cards).
   This is applicable provided the credit card outstanding, as shown on monthly credit card
   statement, is settled fully within 3 days (Grace Period) of payment due date. However, the
   free credit period will not be applicable for cash advance transactions.
   Please note that the free credit period is not valid if any balance of the previous month’s bill is
   outstanding.
